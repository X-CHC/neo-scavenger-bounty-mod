# Neo Scavenger Bounty

**A bounty hunting and contract expansion mod for Neo Scavenger.**

## About This Mod

Neo Scavenger Bounty is a mod designed for players who enjoy challenges, hunting, and taking on dangerous assignments.

The mod introduces a bounty and contract system through the Workforce Center, giving experienced survivors an opportunity to take on dangerous jobs and prove their abilities.

Currently, the mod features one available bounty contract: **Bad Mutha**.

More contracts and additional content are planned for future updates.

## Requirements

This mod requires:

- **Neo Scavenger Extended (NSE)** – Version 743.
- Neo Scavenger – Base game.

Make sure Neo Scavenger Extended is installed and properly configured before using this mod.

## How to Unlock Bounty Contracts

Bounty contracts are not immediately available to every survivor.

To access the bounty dialogue at the **Workforce Center**, you must meet the following requirements:

1. **Strong Trait** – Your character must possess the Strong trait.
2. **Workforce Experience** – You must have purchased at least two Workforce contracts or maintained a Workforce permit for at least two weeks.

Once these requirements are met, the bounty-related dialogue will become available at the Workforce Center.

## Contract System

The mod features a contract-based mission system.

A few things to keep in mind:

- Only one contract can be active at a time.
- Each contract has its own time limit.
- Failing to complete a contract before its deadline will result in failure.
- Completed and failed contracts have different waiting periods before becoming available again.
- Future contracts may introduce different levels of difficulty and risk.

Choose your assignments carefully. Not every contract is suitable for every survivor.
